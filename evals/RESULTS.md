## Method

- Evidence is labelled as verbatim passages and anchored to character offsets in the extracted PDF text, so one set of labels scores every chunking strategy.
- A retrieved chunk is relevant when it overlaps an evidence span by at least half of the shorter of the two. An evidence span is found when the top-k chunks cover at least half of it.
- Precision@k = relevant chunks / k (capped below 1 when a question has fewer relevant chunks than k). Recall@k = found spans / labelled spans. MRR uses the first relevant chunk within the top k.
- Faithfulness: are the answer's claims supported by the retrieved context? Relevance: does it answer the question and agree with the reference answer? Both 1-5, rubric in `evals/judge.py`.
- Per-question rows, including answers and judge reasoning, are in `evals/results.json`.
